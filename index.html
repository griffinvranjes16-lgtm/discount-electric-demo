Winter Spotlight — November

Winter-ready power.

Colder months put more load on your electrical system — space heaters, holiday lights, and furnaces running around the clock. November is the right time to check the system before winter settles in.

  • Safety inspection: catch loose connections, worn wiring, and overloaded circuits early.
  • Panel check: make sure your panel can handle the winter load without tripping.
  • Generator: keep heat and lights on through winter outages.
